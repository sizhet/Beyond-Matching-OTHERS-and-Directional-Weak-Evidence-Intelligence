Beyond Matching: OTHERS, Directional Weak-Evidence Scoring, and Reputational Intelligence (BM-ODWEI) — Initial Public Research Release, v1.1.0 (v1.0.0 Foundation + v1.1.0 Metric Directional Intuition Extension). This theory-first research collection asks how intelligent systems can evaluate information they cannot yet formally match. Its eight foundational papers (BM-001–BM-008) introduce OTHERS as an open space for contextual, relational, reputational, prospective, and other weak or incompletely formalized evidence; Directional Measurement Sails; perspective-dependent evaluation; reputational intelligence; open structural scoring growth; and provenance-aware evidence governance. Six additional papers (MDI-001–MDI-006) extend this foundation with Metric Directional Intuition, investigating Euclidean and metric-native directional scoring, perspective fields and pre-decisional guidance, OTHERS residual scoring and integration with existing matching baselines, lightweight hypothesis testing, reputation calibration, and evidence relay. The integrated framework aims to preserve uncertainty, source dependence, counter-evidence, and human or system decision authority while exploring how validated weak signals might become reusable structural capabilities. It proposes mathematical constructions under stated assumptions, candidate algorithms, research contracts, and testable validation plans; it does not claim demonstrated performance gains, production readiness, or a verified biological account of intuition. This is one combined initial GitHub release tagged v1.1.0; v1.0.0 denotes the foundational research stage, not a separately archived release. Full papers, figures, and release materials: https://github.com/sizhet/Beyond-Matching-OTHERS-and-Directional-Weak-Evidence-Intelligence
