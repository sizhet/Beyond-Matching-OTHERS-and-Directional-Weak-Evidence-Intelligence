Beyond Matching: OTHERS, Directional Weak-Evidence Scoring, and Reputational Intelligence (v1.0.0) explores how intelligent systems can evaluate relevant signals that cannot yet be represented by formal feature matching or conventional distance metrics. The research introduces OTHERS as an open evidence space, Directional Measurement Sails as a proposed mechanism for scoring heterogeneous weak signals across multiple perspectives, and Reputational Intelligence as a framework for assessing indirect and collective evidence while controlling provenance, dependence, uncertainty, and counter-evidence. It further proposes an open structural growth loop through which validated signals may become explicit features, metrics, CCC structures, or principles, while distinguishing evidence assessment from subjective preferences and action selection. This initial release contains eight conceptual and algorithmic research papers and a validation roadmap; its proposed mechanisms have not yet been empirically established.
