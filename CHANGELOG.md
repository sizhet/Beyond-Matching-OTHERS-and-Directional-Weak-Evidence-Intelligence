### Research positioning
- Distinguished **formal matching**, **perspective-dependent assessment**, and **goal-dependent action choice**.
- Proposed **OTHERS** as an open, model-relative evidence space rather than a claim that unexplained signals are intrinsically reliable.
- Proposed **Directional Measurement Sails** as a testable algorithmic design, not a demonstrated performance improvement.
- Defined **Open Structural Scoring Growth** as a candidate pathway from weak signals to validated features, metrics, CCCs, or principles.
- Preserved separation among evidence, perspective, decision, and governance authority.
- Identified related traditions in multi-criteria decision analysis, strategic weak signals, reputation and trust research, and behavioral decision science.

### Validation status
- No empirical superiority, cross-domain generalization, or safety improvement is claimed for v1.0.0.
- Equations, interfaces, and structural-growth procedures are research proposals requiring implementation and controlled testing.
- No runnable demo or production runtime is included in the initial release.

## Versioning policy

- **Patch (1.0.x):** editorial corrections, broken links, metadata, and clarifications that do not change the research architecture.
- **Minor (1.x.0):** new hypotheses, validation results, interface specifications, or backward-compatible research extensions.
- **Major (2.0.0):** substantial architectural revisions or governed runtime integration that changes foundational interfaces or assumptions.
